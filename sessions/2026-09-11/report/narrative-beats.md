## What is deliberately NOT claimed

**The per-match separation formally holds and gets no C007.** Order the seven matches by
pinglamb's APP advantage and yachi's single win (m5, +0.21%) is the smallest, the other six
running +5.57% to +19.57%. That is the 2026-08-19 / 2026-08-25 pattern by shape and not by
evidence: with k wins of m matches the chance ordering puts all k at the bottom is 1/C(m,k) —

reproduction of a one-in-120 finding. Filed with 2026-08-09 as degenerate; the count stays
2 of 11 and the report says why.
