Every recommendation names the claim it rests on. A recommendation with no claim behind it
does not go in the report.

rounds lost    yachi .5629    pinglamb .5729   →  +1.8%   [C002]

Last session the same two lines read +1.8% / +25.4%. So the advice given on 2026-08-09 —

This is worth stating plainly because it is the trap in coaching off a session total. The
session-level APP gap is +10.0% this night and was +18.1% last night; neither number tells
you which regime to work on, and the two nights need opposite answers.

   +7% [C003] against pinglamb's +25% [G071], [G072]. Concretely: stop cashing out early
   when ahead — hold the B2B/combo chain and send in one piece. He already holds heavily

2. **The volume route is spent, two sessions running.** +415 pieces for −206 attack, over
   4% of pinglamb's total [C005]; last session it was −271. Extra pieces only buy the gap
   back if per-piece value holds, and it has not for two nights.
3. **Topouts are the mechanism this time, not a side effect.** 11 of 13 [C006] — his worst
   of six sessions, and he had *zero* last session. More pieces means more stack; the stack
   reached the ceiling eleven times. Clear first, build the setup second, when behind.
4. **Do not chase finesse.** Faults are 1268 to 1262 [G035] and his per-piece perfect rate
   is the better of the two [G047]; KPP is uninformative for both players for the sixth
   consecutive session [G073], [G074].
5. **Match 9 is his own template.** The only match of eleven where his attack per piece beat
   pinglamb's [C019] — and it went 5-2 [G012].

## What NOT to conclude

- Not "yachi got worse". His losing rounds are the *best* they have been relative to
  pinglamb's in six sessions (+1.8%). It is his winning rounds that fell — from a .657-.674
  band across five sessions to .6032.
- Not "the topout advice from 2026-08-01 failed". That column has now been at both extremes
  in consecutive sessions (0 of 4, then 11 of 13). A single session's death tally is not a
  trend; what makes this one worth acting on is that it moved *with* the piece surplus
  [C005], which is a mechanism rather than a coincidence.
- Not "APP is the lever, full stop". It is the lever six sessions running, but *which
  regime carries the gap* moved by 17 points between two consecutive sessions. Re-derive
  the split each time; never coach off the session total.
