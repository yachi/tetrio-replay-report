Read the two lines together: the players are close on their good rounds and far apart on

## yachi

1. **Work the bad rounds, not the good ones.** His won-round attack per piece is ~0.68 and
   his lost-round ~0.55 [G068]; pinglamb's are ~0.72 and ~0.61 [G069]. The won-round gap is
   6-7%, the lost-round gap 11-12% [C002]. His good rounds are already close to competitive;
   what he loses, he loses badly. Concretely: when the board is going wrong, take the
   guaranteed clear instead of holding out for the setup — the objective is to stop the
   round collapsing, not to win it prettily.
2. **The volume route did not pay.** 312 more pieces for 236 less attack, 6-7% of
   pinglamb's total [C005]; pieces 5975 to 5663 [G031], attack 3851 to 3615 [G032]. He is
   faster per round (~1.41 to ~1.35 PPS [G042]) and behind per round on APM (~51.2 to
   ~56.9 [G043]). Extra pieces only buy the gap back if per-piece value holds, and it did
   not.
3. **Do not blame the deaths.** Seven topouts, four his and three pinglamb's [C006]; the
   other 63 rounds ended on garbage [G066]. Stacking out is not the mechanism this time,
   and treating it as one would send him to the wrong drill.
4. **Do not chase inputs either.** KPP 3.628 to 3.639, under 0.3% apart [C008], and he is
   the cheaper of the two [G065]; within each player KPP barely moves between won and lost
   rounds [G070] [G071]. The one hand-skill column he genuinely trails is finesse faults,
   1123 to 976 [G034] — more faults per piece [G045], a lower perfect-piece rate [G046].
   Worth cleaning up, worth ranking below 1 and 2.
5. **Losing a round costs him the next one.** After a lost round he wins the next 12 times
   out of 35 [G052]; pinglamb's same figure is 15 of 25 [G053]. That is a recovery habit,
   and it is trainable separately from anything about stacking.
6. **Match 7 is his own template** — 5-1 [G010], four straight from the start [C015], his
   cleanest of the night, and one of the three matches where the efficiency gap was under
   1% [C007]. Watch how he cashed out in those six rounds.
