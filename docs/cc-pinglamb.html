    What is and isn't shown

    • Every frame was rebuilt independently. Four agents wrote their own checkers from the raw replay data and re-derived all 336 placements (12 clips × 14 pieces × 2 sides): piece shape, empty cells, piece resting on something, cleared rows, garbage rows and hole column, and TETR.IO attack, B2B and combo. Their saved re-runs report 4 mismatch lines, all of one kind: the checker compared the garbage rows Cold Clear inserted on a step with the rows pinglamb received on it, which differ whenever Cold Clear's piece there cleared lines and the rows waited (the rule below). A separate script then rebuilt every frame of both sides from the frames alone, Cold Clear's garbage-waiting rule included, and found nothing wrong. Every caption item (12 titles, 12 summaries and 72 step claims) then went through two skeptics, one checking numbers and one checking wording, in a loop until nothing was refuted: 10 of 192 checks refuted in the first round, then 2, 1 and 0.
    • Garbage: Cold Clear receives exactly the rows pinglamb received, on the same piece, with the same hole. If its piece there clears lines, the rows wait for its next piece that clears nothing, which is TETR.IO's own rule. It gets no credit for cancelling with its attack, and the incoming meter it sees is pinglamb's meter at that moment.
    • Preview: Cold Clear never sees more than pinglamb did: hold plus the 5-piece preview, with one new piece revealed per placement.
    • Not modelled: speed and key inputs. pinglamb played in real time; Cold Clear thought about 0.2 s per piece with no clock. The drop is drawn straight down for clarity, so tucks and spins are shown by where the piece ends up, not how it got there.
    • Cold Clear is a strong bot's line, not "the correct play". It is tuned for Puyo Puyo Tetris scoring, has no 180° spins, and does not know TETR.IO's B2B chain bonus. The bar under pinglamb's field is each move's graded loss against it (taller is worse; orange marks a clear miss of 600 or more; hatched means not graded).
    • Not in the proof chain. Everything on this page comes from a replay simulator and one bot, not from the two independent parsers and Dafny proofs behind the match reports.
